Our Story

Two shopfronts, one promise: your family's health comes first.

Established in 2001 in the heart of Chinhoyi, Lomagundi Pharmacy has proudly served as a cornerstone of the local community for over two decades. From humble beginnings we grew into a trusted household name — and carried that same promise of care through to Harare, where Forestal Machipisa Pharmacy now keeps the Machipisa community stocked and supported from early morning until late at night.

  • Qualified pharmacists on-site at every branch
  • Genuine, quality-assured medicines & products
  • Warm, community-first personal service
  • Two convenient locations across Zimbabwe
