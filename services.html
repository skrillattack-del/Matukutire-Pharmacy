Six Departments, One Convenient Stop

Every branch is organised around the essentials families need most — with knowledgeable staff on hand in every department.

Both Branches
Department 01

Prescriptions & OTC Medicines

Our dispensary is the heart of both branches — accurate, unhurried prescription filling backed by pharmacists who take the time to explain dosage, interactions and what to expect. Alongside that, our over-the-counter shelves cover the everyday essentials: pain relief, cold & flu remedies, vitamins and supplements.

  • Prescription dispensing with pharmacist guidance
  • Cold, flu & pain-relief essentials always in stock
  • Vitamins, supplements & general wellness advice
Both Branches
Department 02

Women's Health & Upkeep

A dedicated aisle for women's everyday wellness — from personal hygiene essentials to healthcare basics like reliable, easy-to-read pregnancy tests. Our staff are discreet, approachable and happy to help you find exactly what you need.

  • Feminine hygiene & personal care essentials
  • Pregnancy tests & maternal wellness basics
  • Friendly, discreet in-store advice
Both Branches
Department 03

Baby & Maternity Care

New parents can find everything in one trip — nappies and wipes from trusted brands, feeding essentials and gentle skin-care basics for delicate skin. We know how precious those early months are, and we're here to make the shopping part easy.

  • Nappies & baby wipes from trusted brands
  • Gentle skin & feeding essentials
  • Friendly advice for new and expecting parents

Optical Care

Our Chinhoyi branch is home to a dedicated Optical section, helping the community with eyewear and vision-care essentials without needing a separate trip across town.
